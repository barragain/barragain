## <img src="assets/icons/bstack.svg" width="22" align="top"> bstack

**[bstack.cc](https://bstack.cc)** is my suite of small browser tools. Type the address and the tool is ready, with no signup. A free account keeps your work on every device.

<a href="https://bstack.cc"><img src="assets/hub.gif" alt="The bstack home page with every tool" width="100%"></a>

### <img src="assets/icons/bpad.svg" width="22" align="top"> [bpad](https://www.bpad.cc) · A notepad for whatever

Open it and start typing. Notes save in your browser as you write, and with an account you can share one and edit it together, live.

<a href="https://www.bpad.cc"><img src="assets/bpad.gif" alt="bpad demo" width="100%"></a>

### <img src="assets/icons/bdrop.svg" width="22" align="top"> [bdrop](https://bdrop.cc) · Phone to computer in one scan

Open it on your computer and scan the code with your phone. Text and files up to 25 MB show up on the other screen, and the drop is deleted within a day.

<a href="https://bdrop.cc"><img src="assets/bdrop.gif" alt="bdrop demo" width="100%"></a>

### <img src="assets/icons/bqrc.svg" width="22" align="top"> [bqrc](https://bqrc.cc) · QR codes that never expire

Type a link and get a QR code you can colour and download. The link sits inside the code itself, so it keeps working with no account and nothing to pay later.

<a href="https://bqrc.cc"><img src="assets/bqrc.gif" alt="bqrc demo" width="100%"></a>

### <img src="assets/icons/btext.svg" width="22" align="top"> [btext](https://btext.cc) · Count and clean any text

Paste any text to count words and characters, or strip the markdown and em dashes AI tools leave behind. Changes shows exactly what was removed.

<a href="https://btext.cc"><img src="assets/btext.gif" alt="btext demo" width="100%"></a>

### <img src="assets/icons/bpdf.svg" width="22" align="top"> [bpdf](https://bpdf.cc) · Compress a PDF to any size

Pick a size and bpdf compresses the PDF to fit, or open the editor to sign and fill it. Without an account, the file never leaves your browser.

<a href="https://bpdf.cc"><img src="assets/bpdf.gif" alt="bpdf demo" width="100%"></a>

### <img src="assets/icons/bpixel.svg" width="22" align="top"> [bpixel](https://bpixel.cc) · HEIC to JPG in your browser

Turn iPhone HEIC photos into JPGs under a size you choose. Location data is stripped by default, and the conversion runs in your browser.

<a href="https://bpixel.cc"><img src="assets/bpixel.gif" alt="bpixel demo" width="100%"></a>

### <img src="assets/icons/bmeet.svg" width="22" align="top"> [bmeet](https://bmeet.cc) · Find a time that works for everyone

Suggest a few times and send the link. People answer Yes, If needed or No without an account, and you confirm the time that works.

<a href="https://bmeet.cc"><img src="assets/bmeet.gif" alt="bmeet demo" width="100%"></a>

### <img src="assets/icons/binvoice.svg" width="22" align="top"> [binvoice](https://binvoice.cc) · An invoice in 60 seconds

Fill in a short form and the invoice builds beside it, ready to download as a PDF. Tax and bank presets cover 222 countries.

<a href="https://binvoice.cc"><img src="assets/binvoice.gif" alt="binvoice demo" width="100%"></a>

### <img src="assets/icons/bsend.svg" width="22" align="top"> [bsend](https://bsend.cc) · Send a file to your other device

Send a file of any size straight from one device to another with a short code. It goes browser to browser, and nothing is stored on a server.

<a href="https://bsend.cc"><img src="assets/bsend.gif" alt="bsend demo" width="100%"></a>

## El Mercado

**[elmercado.com.py](https://elmercado.com.py)** is a free prediction game for Paraguay, in Spanish. You buy Sí or No on questions about football and politics with play coins, and the price moves with what people think. Each month the top ten win real prizes.
